# 🐔 Types of Chicken

Welcome to **Types of Chicken** — a fun and quirky website introducing some of the world's most unusual chickens! Meet unique characters, learn their stories, and enjoy a lighthearted creative experience. 

## 🌟 Featured Characters

### 🍌 Mr. Banana Chicken

The most confused chicken in town! He's on a mission to discover the truth behind his unusual name and find his real parents. Along the way, expect plenty of unexpected adventures (and maybe a little dancing). 

### 💰 Mr. Dollar Chicken

The richest chicken around. He loves gold, enjoys showing off his wealth, and isn't exactly a fan of humans. But beneath all that luxury, there's a story waiting to be told. 

## 🎨 About This Project

This project was created for fun as a creative web design experiment. It combines character design, storytelling, and playful visuals to create an entertaining browsing experience.
